Acme Cloud Services — Company Overview
======================================

Acme Cloud Services (ACS) is a mid-size infrastructure company founded in 2018.
We help startups and mid-market teams run reliable workloads without managing
bare metal themselves.

Core products
-------------
1. Acme Compute — on-demand virtual machines with hourly billing.
2. Acme Object Store — S3-compatible object storage with versioning.
3. Acme Managed Postgres — managed PostgreSQL with automated backups.

Support
-------
Standard support is email-only with a 24-hour response target on business days.
Premium support adds Slack channel access and a 4-hour response target.

Pricing notes
-------------
Compute starts at $0.04 per vCPU-hour. Object storage is $0.02 per GB-month.
Managed Postgres has a free tier of 1 GB RAM / 10 GB disk for evaluation.
