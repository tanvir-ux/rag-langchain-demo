Acme Cloud — Onboarding FAQ
===========================

Q: How do I create an account?
A: Sign up at https://console.acme.example with a work email. Confirm the
   verification link, then create an organization.

Q: Do I need a credit card to start?
A: No. The free Managed Postgres tier and $25 trial credit for Compute do not
   require a card. A card is required only when you leave the free tier.

Q: Where are regions available?
A: us-east-1, us-west-2, and eu-central-1. Data residency options for EU
   customers keep primary copies in eu-central-1.

Q: How do I rotate API keys?
A: In the console under Settings → API Keys → Rotate. Old keys remain valid
   for 24 hours after rotation.

Q: What is the SLA for Premium support?
A: 99.9% monthly uptime credit policy, and a 4-hour first-response target for
   Sev-1 incidents during business hours.
